# 📘 Real Analysis Study Plan (Rudin + Fitzpatrick)

## 🎯 Objective
- Develop real analysis through **formal definitions and proofs** (Rudin).  
- Reinforce with **structured examples and traditional exercises** (Fitzpatrick).  
- Build a foundation for topology, measure theory, and functional analysis.

---

## 📚 Core Texts
- **Walter Rudin – *Principles of Mathematical Analysis*** (*Baby Rudin*, 3rd ed.) – **primary**  
- **Fitzpatrick – *Advanced Calculus*** – **companion for exercises & clarity**  

**Optional:**  
- Bartle & Sherbert – fallback for additional examples if needed  

---

## 🗓️ Structure (40 Weeks)

### 🔹 Phase I: Foundations (Weeks 1–10)
- Sets, functions, logic, fields of reals (**Rudin Ch.1**)  
- Basic topology of ℝ (open, closed, compact, connected) (**Rudin Ch.2**)  
- Numerical sequences and series (**Rudin Ch.3**)  
- Continuous functions, uniform continuity (**Rudin Ch.4**)  

👉 Fitzpatrick Ch.1–4: complements with worked examples  

---

### 🔹 Phase II: Differentiation (Weeks 11–18)
- Differentiability, mean value theorem (**Rudin Ch.5**)  
- Sequences and series of functions (intro, **Rudin Ch.7**)  
- Taylor’s theorem  

👉 Fitzpatrick Ch.5–6: practice and illustrations  

---

### 🔹 Phase III: Integration & Series (Weeks 19–28)
- Riemann–Stieltjes integrals (**Rudin Ch.6**)  
- Uniform convergence, Weierstrass M-test (**Rudin Ch.7**)  
- Power series, exponential/logarithm/trigonometric functions (**Rudin Ch.8**)  

👉 Fitzpatrick Ch.7–9: applied reinforcement  

---

### 🔹 Phase IV: Advanced Topics (Weeks 29–40)
- Metric spaces: completeness, compactness (**Rudin Ch.2 revisited, Ch.7 context**)  
- Multivariable calculus foundations (**Rudin Ch.9**)  
- Implicit/inverse function theorems (**Rudin Ch.9**)  
- Review + exam-style problems (Cambridge past papers if possible)  

👉 Fitzpatrick Ch.10–12: multivariable and advanced calculus  

---

## 🧠 Weekly Template
- **3h** Rudin reading + proofs  
- **3h** Fitzpatrick exercises  
- **1h** summary / cheat sheet  
- **1h** re-prove a key theorem
